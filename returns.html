      Refund & Return Policy

      Every NIKORA order is covered by our 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee. This page explains who is eligible, how to start a return and when your refund arrives.

      30-day money-back window

      You can ask for a refund within 30 days of receiving your order. Contact us or use the return form above and we will confirm whether your item is eligible.

      Eligible items

      Items that are unopened and unused, in their original packaging, with all accessories and the charger included. Used or opened items are not eligible for change-of-mind returns, but items that are defective or damaged are always covered (see below).

      How to start a return

      Use the return form on this page with your Order ID (it starts with #NK-), or email nikora.support@gmail.com. We will reply with return instructions. Please wait for them before sending anything back.

      Return shipping costs (international orders)

      Your order ships from Busan, South Korea (Delivered Korea hub, Suite A03231), so returns travel back to South Korea. For change-of-mind returns, customers are responsible for the international return shipping cost. If your item is defective, damaged in shipping or lost, we cover it in full, including the return shipping, with a free reshipment or a full refund, your choice.

      Customer error: return shipping is non-refundable

      If a parcel cannot be delivered because of an incorrect or incomplete address, unpaid import duties, tariffs or customs fees, or a refused delivery, it is sent back to South Korea at the customer’s expense. The international return shipping cost back to South Korea is non-refundable. Please check your address at checkout and be ready to pay any local duties. See also the Shipping Policy.

      When you get your refund

      After your return arrives and is checked in, we refund the full purchase price to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

      Defective, damaged or lost orders

      Tell us as soon as you notice a problem and include photos if the item is damaged. If tracking has been inactive for more than 14 days, or your parcel has been in transit for more than 30 days, it counts as lost and we reship for free or refund you in full.

      Cancelling an order

      Email us with your order number as soon as possible. If the order has not been dispatched yet, we will cancel it and refund you. After dispatch, you can use the 30-day guarantee instead.

      Need help?

      Our team is available Monday–Sunday, 8am–10pm EST. See the FAQ Center or contact support.
