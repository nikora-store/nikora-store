Section 1

Processing & Dispatch

Every order is bought in South Korea, inspected at our Delivered Korea hub in Busan, then shipped internationally with tracking.

Korean Procurement & Inspection (2–4 business days)

NIKORA buys your items in South Korea (our catalog is referenced from Olive Young Global) and sends them to our logistics partner, the Delivered Korea hub at Suite A03231, Busan. Procurement and inspection take 2–4 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding Korean public holidays). At the hub every item is checked for authenticity, intact seals, expiry dates and damage, then repacked for international transit.

Dispatch Origin: Busan, South Korea

Your parcel leaves from Busan, South Korea, not from a local warehouse in your country, so it crosses an international border and customs rules apply (see Section 4). It ships under the sender name NIKORA Store via FedEx, DHL or K-Packet.

International Shipment Notice
Your order is purchased in Korea and shipped from Busan after inspection. If an order is split into more than one parcel, each parcel has its own tracking number at no additional charge and may arrive on a different day. Shipping is charged only once per order, never per parcel.

Dispatch Confirmation

When a parcel leaves the Busan hub we email you its tracking number and carrier (FedEx, DHL or K-Packet). Parcels sent separately each receive their own tracking number.

Shipping Rates & the $50 Free Shipping Threshold

Express shipping is FREE on orders of $50.00 and above (after any discount), applied to the entire order whether it ships in one parcel or several. Orders under $50.00 pay a flat rate of $4.99, charged once per order, never per package. The shipping line at checkout shows exactly what you pay, and the progress bar in your cart shows how close you are to free shipping.

Carriers: FedEx, DHL & K-Packet

We choose the carrier for each parcel by destination, weight and speed. FedEx and DHL deliver by international express, and K-Packet (Korea Post) carries lighter parcels. All services are tracked end to end, and the carrier cannot be selected at checkout.

Please Note

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees, and can vary with your location, customs checks, carrier capacity and peak seasons. If you order from outside South Korea, which applies to every NIKORA order, customs processing can add time beyond the estimates above.

Section 3

Order Tracking

Follow every package and know exactly what we do if something goes wrong.

Tracking Your Order

Each parcel has its own tracking number, emailed when it is dispatched from Busan. International numbers from FedEx, DHL and K-Packet are all accepted: follow your parcels any time on the Track Order page using your order number (it starts with #NK-) and the email or phone number you ordered with, or paste the carrier tracking number. Tracking updates can take a little time to appear after dispatch.

Split Shipments

If your order ships in two packages, one being delivered before the other is normal. The remaining package is not delayed or lost. Track each package separately.

Delayed, Damaged or Lost Packages

If a package's tracking has been inactive for more than 14 days, or it has been in transit for more than 30 days, it counts as lost. Contact us and we will reship it for free or refund you in full. Items that arrive defective or damaged in shipping are covered in the same way. Protection applies to each package separately.

Section 4

Customs & Taxes

Parcels can be inspected by customs, and local duties may apply.

Customs Inspection

Parcels can be inspected by customs, which may add time to delivery. Delays caused by customs are outside our control and are not counted against the delivery estimates.

Import Duties & Taxes

Depending on your country, import duties or taxes may apply on arrival. These are set by local authorities, not by NIKORA, and must be paid by the recipient. Unpaid duties, tariffs or customs fees, an incorrect address or a refused delivery can lead to the parcel being returned to South Korea; the international return shipping cost is then non-refundable (see the Refund & Return Policy). If you have questions before you order, email nikora.support@gmail.com.

Section 5

Address Corrections

Check your delivery address at checkout. Changes are possible only before dispatch.

Before Your Order Ships

To change or correct your delivery address, email nikora.support@gmail.com with your order number as soon as possible. Orders are processed in 2–4 business days at our Delivered Korea hub, so the sooner you write, the better the chance we can update it in time.

After Dispatch

Once a package is dispatched its address can no longer be edited. In a split order, one package may already be on its way while the other is still processing, so please double-check your address at checkout.
